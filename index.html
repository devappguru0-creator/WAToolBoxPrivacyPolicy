Privacy Policy for WAToolbox

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WAToolbox ("the App") handles information when you use our application.

We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about the information that may be processed when you use WAToolbox.

1. Information We Collect

WAToolbox does not require users to create an account or log in to use the application. We do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, password, or home address.

However, certain third-party services integrated into the App may automatically process certain technical or device-related information according to their own privacy policies.

2. Camera Permission

WAToolbox may request access to your device's camera for features such as QR code scanning.

Camera access is used only when you choose to use a feature that requires the camera. WAToolbox does not use the camera to continuously monitor you or access the camera in the background.

3. Storage and Media Permissions

WAToolbox requests permission to read and write to your device's storage (or media gallery) solely to enable features such as saving and viewing WhatsApp status media (images and videos) and managing downloaded content locally.

All downloaded media files are saved locally on your device. WAToolbox does not collect, upload, or transmit your saved files or personal photos to any external servers.

4. Local Data Storage

WAToolbox may use Android's DataStore or local preferences technology to store certain application settings and preferences locally on your device.

This information is stored locally on your device and is not used to identify you personally.

5. Google AdMob

WAToolbox may use Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob may collect or process certain information, including advertising identifiers, device information, and other information necessary to provide and measure advertisements, in accordance with Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

For more information about how Google handles information, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Firebase Crashlytics

WAToolbox may use Firebase Crashlytics to help us identify and diagnose application crashes and technical issues.

Crashlytics may automatically collect technical information related to application crashes, such as device information, operating system information, application version, and crash logs.

This information is used to help us improve the stability, performance, and reliability of the App.
